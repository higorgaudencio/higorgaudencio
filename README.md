## Hi, I'm Higor

I've spent more than 15 years working with CAD, PDM and PLM, in implementation, pre-sales, technical support and leading technical teams. Today I'm a Solutions Architect at Dassault Systèmes, based in São José dos Campos, Brazil.

Most of what I build comes from the same place: a task people repeat by hand every day, or information that exists but is hard to find. Here you'll find automations, integrations, small tools and experiments. Some are finished, some are still in progress, and some are just me learning something new.

### Things I've been building

**Change history viewer**
Starts from any engineering item and follows its relationships to rebuild the full story of what changed, when and why, on a single timeline.

**Document links from drag and drop**
Drop an object into a Word or LibreOffice document and get a link that opens it back in its source system. One idea, two implementations: an Office add-in and a Python macro.

**Quality dossier generator**
Watches a folder, merges DOCX, XLSX and PDF files into a single PDF with cover, watermark, hash and approval page, and publishes the result automatically.

**Document editing in the browser (proof of concept)**
Testing open source tools (Collabora Online and the WOPI protocol) to edit office files inside a web application.
